7.1 - Circumference and Arc Length

Example 1: A circle has radius \(15\) feet. Find the length of an arc with central angle \(80^\circ\). Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

\(\dfrac{80}{360} \cdot 2\pi(15) = \dfrac{2}{9} \cdot 30\pi = \dfrac{20\pi}{3} \approx 20.94\) ft.

7.2 - Area of Circles and Sectors

Example 2: A sector has radius \(18\) inches and central angle \(50^\circ\). Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

\(\dfrac{50}{360} \cdot \pi(18)^2 = \dfrac{5}{36} \cdot 324\pi = 45\pi \approx 141.37\) square inches.

7.3 - Radians

Example 3: Change \(330^\circ\) to radians. Then find the arc length for \(r = 20\) and \(\theta = \dfrac{2\pi}{5}\).

\(330 \cdot \dfrac{\pi}{180} = \dfrac{11\pi}{6}\). Then \(s = r\theta = 20 \cdot \dfrac{2\pi}{5} = 8\pi \approx 25.13\).

7.4 - Inscribed Angles and Tangents

Example 4: An inscribed quadrilateral has \(m\angle B = (3x + 9)^\circ\) and \(m\angle D = (7x - 29)^\circ\). Find \(x\) and both angles.

Opposite angles add to \(180^\circ\): \((3x + 9) + (7x - 29) = 180\), so \(10x - 20 = 180\) and \(x = 20\). The angles are \(69^\circ\) and \(111^\circ\).

7.5 - The Equation of a Circle

Example 5: Write \(x^2 + y^2 - 10x + 4y + 13 = 0\) in standard form. Find the center and radius.

\((x^2 - 10x + 25) + (y^2 + 4y + 4) = -13 + 25 + 4\), so \((x - 5)^2 + (y + 2)^2 = 16\). The center is \((5, -2)\) and the radius is \(4\).

✍️ Practice On Your Own

7.1 - I can find circumference and arc length, and solve for arcs and radii.

  1. A circle has radius \(7.5\) inches. Find its circumference. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  2. A circle has a circumference of \(62.8\) centimeters. Find its radius. Round to the nearest tenth.

  3. Find the length of an arc with central angle \(100^\circ\) on a circle with radius \(18\) meters. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  4. A circle is cut into three arcs measuring \(3x^\circ\), \((5x - 20)^\circ\), and \((2x + 40)^\circ\). Find \(x\) and each arc.

  5. A wheel has a diameter of \(28\) inches. How far does it roll in \(10\) turns? Round to the nearest tenth.

  6. An arc of \(120^\circ\) is \(8\pi\) centimeters long. Find the radius.

7.2 - I can find the area of a circle, a sector, and a shaded region.

  1. Find the area of a circle with diameter \(18\) feet. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  2. A sector has radius \(9\) meters and central angle \(280^\circ\). Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  3. A \(40^\circ\) sector has an area of \(10\pi\) square inches. Find the radius. Round to the nearest hundredth.

  4. A ring has an outer radius of \(12\) and an inner radius of \(7\). Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  5. A circle with a diameter of \(14\) feet is cut out of a square with sides of \(14\) feet. Find the shaded area. Round to the nearest hundredth.

  6. A 9-inch pizza costs \(\$7\). A 13-inch pizza costs \(\$13\). Which is the better deal? Round to the nearest hundredth.

7.3 - I can change between degrees and radians and use \(s = r\theta\) and \(A = \frac{1}{2}r^2\theta\).

  1. Change \(72^\circ\) to radians. Give the exact answer.

  2. Change \(\dfrac{11\pi}{12}\) radians to degrees.

  3. Change \(4\) radians to degrees. Round to the nearest hundredth.

  4. \(r = 9\) and \(\theta = \dfrac{5\pi}{6}\). Find the arc length and the sector area. Give exact answers and decimals rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  5. A sector has radius \(10\) and area \(25\pi\). Find \(\theta\) in radians and in degrees.

7.4 - I can use inscribed angles, inscribed quadrilaterals, and tangent lines.

  1. An inscribed angle measures \(57^\circ\). Find its intercepted arc.

  2. An inscribed angle measures \((5x - 8)^\circ\) and its intercepted arc measures \((12x - 36)^\circ\). Find \(x\) and both measures.

  3. \(\overline{AC}\) is a diameter and \(B\) is on the circle. \(m\angle A = (6x)^\circ\) and \(m\angle C = (3x)^\circ\). Find \(x\) and both angles.

  4. An inscribed quadrilateral has \(m\angle A = (7x + 8)^\circ\) and \(m\angle C = (5x + 16)^\circ\). Find \(x\) and both angles.

  5. \(\overline{PT}\) is tangent to a circle at \(T\). The radius is \(12\) and \(PT = 16\). Find \(OP\).

  6. Two tangent segments from one point measure \((6x - 5)\) and \((2x + 19)\). Find \(x\) and the length.

  7. A circle has center \((0, 0)\) and radius \(10\). A tangent line touches it at \(T(-6, 8)\). Find the slope of the radius and the slope of the tangent.

7.5 - I can write, read, and use the equation of a circle.

  1. Find the center and radius of \((x - 7)^2 + (y + 3)^2 = 40\). Give the radius as a square root and as a decimal rounded to the nearest hundredth.

  2. Write the equation of the circle with center \((-5, 2)\) and radius \(6\).

  3. Write the equation of the circle with center \((4, 4)\) that passes through \((10, 12)\).

  4. A diameter has endpoints \((-4, -2)\) and \((8, 6)\). Write the equation of the circle.

  5. Write \(x^2 + y^2 + 10x - 6y - 2 = 0\) in standard form. Find the center and radius.

  6. Is \((5, 9)\) on, inside, or outside the circle \((x - 1)^2 + (y - 4)^2 = 40\)? Show your work.

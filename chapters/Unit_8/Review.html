8.1 - Area of Polygons and Composite Figures

Example 1: A regular hexagon has sides of \(6\) in. Find its area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

The apothem is the long leg of a \(30^\circ\)-\(60^\circ\)-\(90^\circ\) triangle with short leg \(3\), so \(a = 3\sqrt{3}\). The perimeter is \(36\). \(A = \dfrac{1}{2}aP = \dfrac{1}{2}(3\sqrt{3})(36) = 54\sqrt{3} \approx 93.5\) square inches.

8.2 - Cross Sections and Solids of Revolution

Example 2: A solid has \(8\) faces and \(12\) vertices. How many edges does it have?

Euler’s formula: \(F + V - E = 2\). So \(8 + 12 - E = 2\) and \(E = 18\).

8.3 - Surface Area and Nets

Example 3: A cylinder has radius \(5\) ft and height \(12\) ft. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

\(SA = 2\pi r^2 + 2\pi r h = 2\pi(25) + 2\pi(5)(12) = 50\pi + 120\pi = 170\pi \approx 534.1\) square feet.

8.4 - Volume of Prisms and Cylinders

Example 4: A tank is \(20\) ft by \(12\) ft by \(5\) ft. Find its volume in cubic feet and in cubic yards. Round the yards to the nearest tenth.

\(V = 20 \cdot 12 \cdot 5 = 1200\) ft\(^3\). \(1200 \div 27 \approx 44.4\) yd\(^3\).

8.5 - Volume of Pyramids, Cones, and Spheres

Example 5: A cone has radius \(6\) and slant height \(10\). Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

\(h = \sqrt{10^2 - 6^2} = 8\). \(V = \dfrac{1}{3}\pi(36)(8) = 96\pi \approx 301.6\) cubic units.

8.6 - Density and Modeling

Example 6: A model tank is \(\dfrac{1}{10}\) of the size of a real tank. The real tank holds \(5000\) gallons. How much does the model hold?

Volume scales by \(k^3 = \left(\dfrac{1}{10}\right)^3 = \dfrac{1}{1000}\). The model holds \(5000 \div 1000 = 5\) gallons.

✍️ Practice On Your Own

8.1 - I can find the area of polygons and composite figures.

  1. A parallelogram has a base of \(14\) cm and a height of \(9\) cm. Find its area.

  2. A triangle has vertices \((1, 1)\), \((9, 1)\) and \((5, 7)\). Find its area in square units.

  3. A trapezoid has bases \(11\) in and \(17\) in and a height of \(8\) in. Find its area.

  4. A regular hexagon has sides of \(4\) m and an apothem of about \(3.46\) m. Find its area to the nearest tenth.

  5. A wall is \(20\) ft wide and \(9\) ft tall. It has a door \(3\) ft by \(7\) ft and a window \(4\) ft by \(3\) ft. One gallon of paint covers \(350\) ft\(^2\). How much wall must be painted? How many whole gallons are needed?

  6. A triangle has an area of \(54\) in\(^2\) and a base of \(12\) in. Find its height.

8.2 - I can name solids, describe cross sections, and spin shapes into solids.

  1. A pentagonal prism has \(7\) faces and \(10\) vertices. How many edges does it have? Check with \(F + V - E = 2\).

  2. A solid has \(6\) faces and \(8\) vertices. How many edges does it have?

  3. A plane cuts a square pyramid parallel to its base. What shape is the cross section?

  4. Name the cross section: (a) a plane through the axis of a cylinder, (b) any plane through the center of a sphere.

  5. A rectangle \(4\) cm wide and \(7\) cm tall spins about its \(7\) cm side. Name the solid. Give its radius and height.

  6. A right triangle with legs \(5\) and \(12\) spins about the leg of length \(5\). Name the solid. Give its radius, height and slant height.

8.3 - I can find the surface area of solids.

  1. A box is \(9\) m by \(4\) m by \(3\) m. Find its surface area.

  2. A triangular prism has a right-triangle base with legs \(6\) and \(8\) and hypotenuse \(10\). The prism is \(15\) long. Find its surface area.

  3. A cylinder has radius \(3\) ft and height \(10\) ft. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  4. A square pyramid has a base side of \(10\) in and a slant height of \(13\) in. Find its surface area.

  5. A cone has radius \(7\) cm and slant height \(25\) cm. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  6. A sphere has a diameter of \(18\) in. Find its surface area. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

8.4 - I can find the volume of prisms and cylinders.

  1. A cylinder has a volume of \(245\pi\) cm\(^3\) and a radius of \(7\) cm. Find its height.

  2. A triangular prism has a triangle base with base \(10\) and height \(6\). The prism is \(9\) long. Find its volume.

  3. A cylinder has radius \(4\) m and height \(11\) m. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  4. A slanted cylinder has radius \(5\) in, a slant length of \(15\) in and a vertical height of \(12\) in. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  5. A concrete slab is \(24\) ft by \(15\) ft and \(6\) in thick. Find its volume in cubic yards (\(27\) ft\(^3\) = \(1\) yd\(^3\)). Round up to the next whole yard for the order.

  6. A pool is \(30\) ft by \(15\) ft and the water is \(5\) ft deep. How many gallons does it hold? Use \(1\) ft\(^3 \approx 7.48\) gallons.

8.5 - I can find the volume of pyramids, cones, spheres, and composite solids.

  1. A square pyramid has a base side of \(12\) and a height of \(9\). Find its volume.

  2. A cone has radius \(9\) cm and height \(10\) cm. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  3. A square pyramid has a base side of \(16\) and a slant height of \(17\). Find its height, then its volume.

  4. A sphere has radius \(6\) in. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  5. A hemisphere has radius \(9\) ft. Find its volume. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

  6. A cylinder with radius \(3\) and height \(8\) has a cone on top with radius \(3\) and height \(4\). Find the volume of the solid. Give the exact answer and a decimal rounded to the nearest tenth.

8.6 - I can use density, scale, and modeling to solve real problems.

  1. A concrete slab is \(20\) ft by \(10\) ft and \(4\) in thick. Concrete weighs \(150\) lb per cubic foot. Find the weight of the slab to the nearest pound.

  2. An object has a mass of \(360\) g and a volume of \(400\) cm\(^3\). Find its density. Does it float in water?

  3. A city has \(150{,}000\) people in \(60\) square miles. Find its population density.

  4. A small box has a volume of \(30\) cm\(^3\). A similar box is \(4\) times as long in every direction. Find the volume of the large box. By what factor does its surface area grow?

  5. A model ship is {{f 1/50}} of the size of the real ship. The real ship has a hull area of \(5000\) ft\(^2\) and a volume of \(200{,}000\) ft\(^3\). Find the model’s hull area and volume.

  6. Two cans each hold \(300\) cm\(^3\). Can A has radius \(3\) cm. Can B has radius \(5\) cm. Which uses less metal? Round to the nearest tenth.

    B. \(40\) cm\(^3\)

  8. A block has a mass of \(900\) g and a volume of \(600\) cm\(^3\). Water has a density of \(1\) g per cm\(^3\). What happens when the block is put in water?

    A. It floats, because its density is \(0.67\) g per cm\(^3\).
    B. It floats, because its density is \(1.5\) g per cm\(^3\).
    C. It sinks, because its density is \(1.5\) g per cm\(^3\).
    D. It sinks, because its density is \(0.67\) g per cm\(^3\).

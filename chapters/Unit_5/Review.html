👥 Learn Together

5.1 - Ratios and Proportions

Example 1: a. Solve \(\dfrac{x - 3}{4} = \dfrac{9}{x - 3}\).

  1. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \(3 : 4 : 5\). Find each angle.

  2. \((x - 3)^2 = 36\), so \(x - 3 = 6\) or \(x - 3 = -6\). \(x = 9\) or \(x = -3\).

  3. \(3x + 4x + 5x = 180\), so \(x = 15\). The angles are \(45^\circ\), \(60^\circ\), and \(75^\circ\).

5.2 - Similar Figures and Scale Factor

Example 2: \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF\). \(AB = 8\) and \(DE = 12\). The perimeter of \(\triangle ABC\) is 30 and its area is 40 square units. Find \(k\) from \(\triangle ABC\) to \(\triangle DEF\). Then find the perimeter and area of \(\triangle DEF\).

\(k = \dfrac{12}{8} = 1.5\). Perimeter: \(1.5 \cdot 30 = 45\). Area: \(1.5^2 \cdot 40 = 90\) square units.

5.3 - Similar Triangles

Example 3: \(AB = 9\), \(AC = 12\), and \(m\angle A = 55^\circ\). \(DE = 6\), \(DF = 8\), and \(m\angle D = 55^\circ\). Are the triangles similar? Write the statement.

\(\dfrac{6}{9} = \dfrac{2}{3}\) and \(\dfrac{8}{12} = \dfrac{2}{3}\). The angles are between the sides. Similar by SAS. \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF\).

5.4 - Triangle Proportionality

Example 4: a. \(\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}\). \(AD = 5\), \(DB = x\), \(AE = 6\), and \(EC = 9\). Find \(x\).

  1. A midsegment is \(2x - 1\) long. The side it is parallel to is \(3x + 5\) long. Find \(x\).

  2. \(\dfrac{5}{x} = \dfrac{6}{9}\), so \(6x = 45\) and \(x = 7.5\).

  3. \(3x + 5 = 2(2x - 1)\), so \(3x + 5 = 4x - 2\) and \(x = 7\). The midsegment is 13 and the side is 26.

5.5 - Indirect Measurement

Example 5: A person 6 ft tall casts a shadow 4.5 ft long. At the same time a flagpole casts a shadow 27 ft long. How tall is the flagpole?

\(\dfrac{6}{4.5} = \dfrac{h}{27}\), so \(4.5h = 162\) and \(h = 36\) ft.

✍️ Practice On Your Own

5.1 - I can write and solve proportions, use scales, and split a total in a ratio.

  1. Write the ratio of 24 inches to 4 feet in lowest terms.

  2. Solve \(\dfrac{7}{x} = \dfrac{14}{9}\).

  3. Solve \(\dfrac{x + 4}{9} = \dfrac{x}{6}\).

  4. A map has the scale \(1 \text{ cm} : 6 \text{ km}\). Two towns are 8.5 cm apart on the map. How far apart are they?

  5. The angles of a triangle are in the ratio \(2 : 2 : 5\). Find each angle.

  6. Amira solves \(\dfrac{4}{x} = \dfrac{6}{15}\). She writes \(4 \cdot 6 = 15x\) and gets \(x = 1.6\). Find her mistake and solve it correctly.

5.2 - I can decide whether figures are similar and use the scale factor.

  1. Are a 5 in by 8 in photo and a 15 in by 24 in photo similar? If so, give \(k\).

  2. \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle PQR\). Which angle matches \(\angle B\)? Which side matches \(\overline{AC}\)?

  3. \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle DEF\). \(AB = 14\), \(DE = 21\), and \(BC = 10\). Find \(EF\).

  4. The scale factor from a large figure to a small figure is \(\dfrac{1}{3}\). The large figure has area 63 and perimeter 45. Find the area and perimeter of the small figure.

  5. Mohammed says any two rhombuses are similar because all their sides are equal. Give a counterexample.

5.3 - I can decide whether two triangles are similar and find missing sides.

  1. \(\triangle ABC\): \(m\angle A = 40^\circ\), \(m\angle B = 75^\circ\). \(\triangle DEF\): \(m\angle D = 40^\circ\), \(m\angle F = 65^\circ\). Are they similar? Write the statement.

  2. Triangle 1 has sides 6, 10, 14. Triangle 2 has sides 9, 15, 21. Are they similar?

  3. \(AB = 4\), \(AC = 6\), \(m\angle A = 30^\circ\). \(DE = 6\), \(DF = 10\), \(m\angle D = 30^\circ\). Are the triangles similar?

  4. \(\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}\), \(AD = 6\), \(DB = 3\), and \(DE = 10\). Find \(BC\).

  5. \(A(0, 0)\), \(B(3, 0)\), \(C(0, 4)\) and \(D(1, 1)\), \(E(7, 1)\), \(F(1, 9)\). Use distances to show the triangles are similar. Find \(k\).

5.4 - I can use parallel lines and midsegments in triangles.

  1. \(\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}\), \(AD = 8\), \(DB = 12\), and \(AE = 10\). Find \(EC\).

  2. \(AD = 4\), \(DB = 6\), \(AE = 6\), and \(EC = 9\). Is \(\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}\)?

  3. A midsegment is 11 cm long. How long is the side it is parallel to?

  4. A midsegment is \(3x + 2\) long. The side it is parallel to is \(8x - 6\) long. Find \(x\) and the midsegment.

  5. Three parallel lines cut two transversals. On one, the parts are 8 and 12. On the other, the first part is 10. Find the second part.

5.5 - I can use similar triangles to measure heights and distances.

  1. A 1.5 m stick has a shadow 2 m long. A tower casts a shadow 50 m long at the same time. How tall is the tower?

  2. Eye height 1.6 m. The person is 2.4 m from a mirror. A tree is 18 m from the mirror. How tall is the tree?

  3. River stakes: \(BC = 18\) m, \(CD = 6\) m, and \(DE = 7\) m. Find the width \(AB\).

  4. A person 5 ft tall stands 8 ft from a lamp. Her shadow is 4 ft long. How tall is the lamp?

Test practice

  1. A map has the scale 1 inch to 25 miles. Two cities are 4.5 inches apart on the map. What is the real distance between the cities?

    A. 18 mi
    B. 29.5 mi
    C. 112.5 mi
    D. 125 mi

  2. \(\triangle ABC \sim \triangle XYZ\). Which statement must be true?

    A. \(\angle A \cong \angle Y\)
    B. \(\dfrac{AB}{XY} = \dfrac{BC}{YZ}\)
    C. \(AB = XY\)
    D. \(\angle B \cong \angle Z\)

  3. Two similar figures have scale factor 4 from the small one to the large one. The area of the small figure is 5 square feet. What is the area of the large figure?

    A. 20 square feet
    B. 40 square feet
    C. 80 square feet
    D. 160 square feet

  4. In \(\triangle ABC\) and \(\triangle DEF\), \(AB = 6\), \(BC = 9\), \(m\angle B = 70^\circ\), \(DE = 4\), \(EF = 6\), and \(m\angle E = 70^\circ\). Which rule shows that the triangles are similar?

    A. AA
    B. SSS
    C. SAS
    D. The triangles are not similar.

  5. In \(\triangle ABC\), \(\overline{DE} \parallel \overline{BC}\), \(AD = 6\), \(DB = 2\), and \(AE = 9\). What is \(EC\)?

    A. 1
    B. 3
    C. 4.5
    D. 27

  6. A midsegment of a triangle is 14 inches long. How long is the side that is parallel to it?

    A. 7 in
    B. 14 in
    C. 28 in
    D. 56 in

  7. A person 5 ft tall has a shadow 4 ft long. At the same time a building has a shadow 60 ft long. How tall is the building?

    A. 48 ft
    B. 60 ft
    C. 75 ft
    D. 300 ft
